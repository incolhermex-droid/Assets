D&D 5.5e ALL-IN-ONE BEYOND — THE WORLD UPDATE

Baldur's Gate 3 was built on D&D 2014 rules. This mod updates the entire game to the official 2024 Player's Handbook (5.5e).

■ Every base class fully rebuilt from the ground up
■ Three brand new full classes added (Gunslinger, Illrigger, Artificer)
■ 80+ feats from five different sourcebooks
■ Reworked races and 37 backgrounds with Origin Feats
■ 60+ new and revised spells
■ New weapons, items and unique loot
■ All in one standalone mod — no dependencies required

────────────────────────

CORE RULES — MAJOR CHANGES

■ Bloodied Condition: Creatures at or below 50% HP become Bloodied. Many abilities interact with this state.
■ Heavy Armor: Requires Strength 13 to move effectively.
■ One Spell Slot per Turn: No more double-casting with Action + Bonus Action.
■ Weapon Drawing: Equip or draw weapons as part of your Attack action.
■ Prone Rework: Standing up costs half your Speed.
■ Extra Attack + War Magic support for certain classes.
■ Heavy Weapon Ability Requirements added.

────────────────────────

CLASS OVERHAUL — 12 BASE CLASSES

All subclasses now unlock at Level 3.

Barbarian – Primal Knowledge, Brutal Strike, Instinctive Pounce
Bard – Font of Inspiration, reworked Magical Secrets
Cleric – Divine Order, Sear Undead, Blessed Strikes
Druid – Primal Order, Wild Resurgence, Elemental Fury
Fighter – Tactical Mind, Tactical Shift, Tactical Master
Monk – Monk's Focus (replaces Ki), Deflect Attacks
Paladin – Divine Smite as a prepared spell, Faithful Steed
Ranger – Reworked Favored Enemy, Deft Explorer
Rogue – Cunning Strike & Improved Cunning Strike
Sorcerer – Innate Sorcery, Sorcery Incarnate
Warlock – Invocations from Level 1 + 20+ options
Wizard – Ritual Adept, Scholar

NEW CLASSES

GUNSLINGER
A martial class centered on firearms and the Risk resource. High-risk, high-reward gameplay.
Subclasses: High Roller, Spellslinger, White Hat

ILLRIGGER
Infernal knights who dominate the battlefield using a unique Seal system.
Subclasses: Hellspeaker (Moloch), Painkiller (Dispater), Sanguine Knight (Sutekh), Shadowmaster

ARTIFICER
Masters of magical invention and item crafting.
Subclasses: Alchemist, Armorer, Artillerist

────────────────────────

WEAPON MASTERY
New tactical layer for all martial characters from Level 1.
Properties include: Cleave, Graze, Topple, Nick, Vex, Slow, Push, Sap, Flex.
