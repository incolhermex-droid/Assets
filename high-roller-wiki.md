## What Is the High Roller?

The High Roller is a **Gunslinger archetype** built around fortune, bluffing, and calculated chaos. A gambler, a risk-taker, a manipulator of luck itself — the High Roller doesn't just fight, they **play the odds**. Whether at the card table or the battlefield, they always have one more trick up their sleeve.

This archetype takes the base class's **Risk** mechanic and pushes it further — bigger dice, better outcomes, and a Level 10 capstone that turns a lucky roll into a bonus attack.

## Level 3 Features

### 🎭 Poker Face

*You've learned to read people the way others read books.*

Gain proficiency in **Insight** and **Deception**. When you make a Charisma (Deception) check, add your **Wisdom modifier** as a bonus.

> *Poker Face stacks two skills that are normally opposed — reading people and lying to them — into one cohesive toolkit. The Wisdom bonus to Deception means your perceptiveness directly fuels your ability to bluff.*

---

### 🎲 Liar's Dice

*Confidence is a weapon. You've sharpened yours.*

Once per **Short Rest**, you can force a creature that can see you to make a **Wisdom saving throw**. On a failure, they are **Charmed** by you until the end of your next turn.

> *Liar's Dice is a single-target social/combat hybrid tool — a free Charm attempt that costs no spell slot and refreshes on Short Rest. Use it to neutralize a dangerous attacker for a turn, or to open a social encounter on your terms.*

## Level 6 Features

### 🃏 Risky Business

*The rules of the house just changed. You wrote the new ones.*

Your **Risk** feature now adds a **d8 instead of a d6**, and on a result of 1, you no longer subtract — you simply add nothing. The house rules are yours now.

> *Risky Business is a pure upgrade to your core resource with zero downside added. The floor goes from "lose value" to "no value" — meaning every Risk roll from Level 6 onward is either neutral or beneficial. This single change makes Risk dramatically safer to use every single turn.*

![Gunslinger Divider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/incolhermex-droid/Assets/refs/heads/main/gunslinger-divider.svg)

## Level 10 Features

### 💰 Risk Taker

*The moment the odds flip in your favor — that's the moment you live for.*

When you use **Risk** and roll a **4 or higher**, you can make **one additional firearm attack** as a **Bonus Action**.

> *Risk Taker turns your highest-value Risk outcomes into extra damage on top of the bonus you already gained. At Level 10, a good Risk roll isn't just "more damage on this attack" — it's an entirely free follow-up shot. Combined with Extra Attack from Level 5, a great turn can mean three or more firearm attacks.*

![Gunslinger Divider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/incolhermex-droid/Assets/refs/heads/main/gunslinger-divider.svg)

## 🎯 Playstyle Summary

The High Roller is a **variance-embracing striker** that turns the base Gunslinger's gamble mechanic into the centerpiece of every turn.

**Your combat loop:**
1. **Attack + Risk** — every attack rolls a Risk die; from Level 6, the worst case is "no bonus," never "penalty"
2. **Liar's Dice** — open dangerous encounters by Charming the biggest threat for a turn
3. **Poker Face** — out-of-combat, you're the party's best liar and best lie detector simultaneously
4. **Risk Taker** at Level 10 — every Risk roll of 4+ becomes a bonus shot; the math compounds fast

> *Other Gunslingers calculate. The High Roller bets — and by Level 6, the bet is rigged in their favor.*
