D&D 5.5e ALL-IN-ONE BEYOND — THE WORLD UPDATE

BG3 was built on D&D 2014 rules.

■ Every base class rebuilt from scratch.

■ 80+ feats across five sourcebooks.
■ New races, backgrounds, spells, and items.
■ All in one standalone mod. No dependencies.

------------------------

CORE RULES — REBUILT

The 2024 PHB changes how the game works at its foundation.

■ BLOODIED — Creatures at or below 50% HP gain the Bloodied condition. Many abilities interact with this state.
■ HEAVY ARMOR STRENGTH REQUIREMENT — Strength 13 required to wear Heavy Armor effectively. Low-STR casters can no longer wear plate for free.
■ ONE SPELL SLOT PER TURN — You can only expend one spell slot per turn. No more double-casting.
■ EQUIPPING WEAPONS — Draw or equip a weapon as part of your Attack action.
■ PRONE REWORK — Standing costs half your Speed. Melee attacks have Advantage, ranged attacks have Disadvantage.
■ HIDING REWORK — Stealth updated to 2024 standards.
■ EXTRA ATTACK + WAR MAGIC — Certain classes can now mix spells and attacks in the same Extra Attack sequence.
■ HEAVY WEAPON ABILITY REQUIREMENT — Heavy weapons require appropriate ability scores.

GUNSLINGER

Risk is in a Gunslinger's blood. Bold renegades who forge their own path with dangerous firearms — surviving by wits, split-second timing, and a considerable amount of luck.

A full martial class built around firearms and the RISK resource. Weapon Mastery at Level 1.

Core Features:
■ Firearm Expert (Lvl 1)
■ Quick Draw (Lvl 1)
■ Critical Shot (Lvl 2)
■ Risk (Lvl 2)
■ Gut Shot (Lvl 5)
■ Evasion (Lvl 7)
■ Overkill (Lvl 11)

Subclasses:
■ HIGH ROLLER — Push your luck. Poker Face, Liar's Dice, Risky Business.
■ SPELLSLINGER — Magic meets gunfire. Bang You're Dead, Spellshot, Counter-Mage.
■ WHITE HAT — The lawful gunslinger. Lay Down the Law, Steely-Eyed, Long Arm of the Law.

WEAPON MASTERY — NEW COMBAT LAYER

Every martial character unlocks Weapon Mastery at Level 1.
Each weapon type has a unique special property.

■ CLEAVE — Hit a second adjacent creature on a successful attack.
■ GRAZE — Deal ability modifier damage even on a miss.
■ TOPPLE — Force a CON save or knock the target Prone.
■ NICK — Light weapons grant an extra attack without using your Bonus Action.
■ VEX — Gain Advantage on your next attack against the same target.
■ SLOW — Reduce target Speed by 10 ft until your next turn.
■ PUSH — Push the target 10 ft away.
■ SAP — Target has Disadvantage on its next attack roll.
■ FLEX — Use one-handed or two-handed for different damage dice.
