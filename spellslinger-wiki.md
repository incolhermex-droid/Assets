## What Is the Spellslinger?

The Spellslinger is a **Gunslinger archetype** that fuses arcane spellcasting with firearms. Not all Gunslingers walk the mundane path — the Spellslinger has unlocked a secret most arcane scholars refuse to acknowledge: a firearm can be a spellcasting focus, and a bullet can be a spell delivery system. Every shot becomes a question — is it gunpowder, magic, or both?

This is the only Gunslinger archetype with real **spellcasting**, and the only Warlock-list proxy among the new classes that channels spells specifically *through* a weapon rather than alongside it.

## Level 3 Features

### 📖 Spellcasting

*Your firearm doesn't replace your spellbook. It is your spellbook.*

You gain the ability to cast spells, using **Intelligence** as your spellcasting ability. Your firearm serves as your **spellcasting focus** — no separate component pouch or arcane focus required.

> *This is the foundation that makes the rest of the archetype work. Intelligence as the casting stat sets the Spellslinger apart from the base Gunslinger's Dexterity/Wisdom focus — a genuine secondary stat investment for genuine secondary capability.*

---

### 🔫 Bang, You're Dead!

*The spell and the bullet arrive at the same time. That's the whole point.*

When you cast a spell that requires a **spell attack roll**, you can make it through your firearm instead. On a hit, the spell deals its damage **and** your firearm's damage.

> *Bang, You're Dead! is the signature fusion mechanic. A single attack roll resolves both your spell and your weapon simultaneously — meaning a successful hit deals double the damage sources of a normal spell attack. This makes every offensive spell with an attack roll component dramatically more attractive for a Spellslinger than for any other caster.*

![Gunslinger Divider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/incolhermex-droid/Assets/refs/heads/main/gunslinger-divider.svg)

## Level 6 Features

### ✨ Spellshot

*The magic and the metal are one.*

Once per turn, when you hit a creature with a firearm attack, you can expend a **spell slot** to deal extra **force damage** equal to **1d8 per spell slot level**.

> *Spellshot converts unused spell slots directly into weapon damage — a Smite-equivalent for the Spellslinger. A Level 3 slot adds 3d8 force damage on top of your firearm hit. Unlike Bang, You're Dead!, this doesn't require casting a spell with an attack roll — it works on any successful firearm hit, making it far more flexible turn to turn.*

![Gunslinger Divider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/incolhermex-droid/Assets/refs/heads/main/gunslinger-divider.svg)

## Level 10 Features

### 🛡️ Counter-Mage

*You don't counterspell. You just shoot the caster before the spell finishes forming.*

When a creature you can see **casts a spell**, you can use your **Reaction** to make a firearm attack against them. On a hit, they must make a **Constitution saving throw** or their spell **fails** and the spell slot is **wasted**.

> *Counter-Mage is functionally a ranged Counterspell with a twist — it's an attack roll followed by a saving throw, not a single contested check. Against enemy casters, this is one of the most disruptive Level 10 features in the mod: a failed save means the enemy spent a turn and a spell slot for nothing, while you dealt firearm damage and used only your Reaction.*

![Gunslinger Divider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/incolhermex-droid/Assets/refs/heads/main/gunslinger-divider.svg)

## 🎯 Playstyle Summary

The Spellslinger is a **hybrid caster-marksman** that makes every offensive option — spell or shot — feed into the other.

**Your combat loop:**
1. **Bang, You're Dead!** — lead with attack-roll spells fired through your firearm for double damage sources
2. **Spellshot** — on any other firearm hit, dump a spell slot for bonus force damage
3. **Extra Attack** (from base class, Level 5) — stacks with both fusion mechanics for a full turn of layered damage
4. **Counter-Mage** at Level 10 — punish enemy spellcasters the instant they commit to a spell

> *Other casters worry about losing concentration in melee. The Spellslinger doesn't have that problem — their best spells are delivered at the speed of a bullet.*
