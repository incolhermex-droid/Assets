## What Is the Warlock?

The Warlock is a **Charisma-based spellcaster** powered by a pact with an otherworldly entity — an archfey, a fiend, a great old one, or something stranger still. Unlike other spellcasters who draw on internal reserves or divine favor, the Warlock's power comes from **negotiation**. The patron grants magic. The Warlock uses it.

Rebuilt for the 2024 PHB, the Warlock now gains **Eldritch Invocations from Level 1** — 20+ available options including all three Pact Boons. **Magical Cunning** at Level 2 lets you recover half your Pact Magic slots on a Short Rest. **Mystic Arcanum** at Level 11 adds a high-level spell slot usable once per Long Rest. And the subclass — your **Otherworldly Patron** — unlocks from Level 1, meaning your pact identity is present from the first moment of play.

The Warlock is also the only class in the mod with **Shadow Blade** added to its spell list — a Level 2 psychic weapon spell that creates a sword of solidified shadow.

### 🌑 Pact Magic

The Warlock's spellcasting works differently from every other caster. You have **very few spell slots** — but they are always at your **highest available spell level**, and they recover on a **Short Rest**.

| WARLOCK LEVEL | SPELL SLOTS | SLOT LEVEL |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 1st |
| 2–4 | 2 | 1st |
| 5–6 | 2 | 3rd |
| 7–8 | 2 | 4th |
| 9–10 | 2 | 5th |
| 11+ | 3 | 5th |

> *Pact Magic rewards Short Rests. A Warlock who rests between encounters fights at full power every time. In an adventuring day with multiple Short Rests, the Warlock's effective spell output matches or exceeds other casters.*

---

### ✨ Eldritch Invocations — From Level 1

The Warlock gains **Eldritch Invocations** starting at **Level 1** — permanent magical abilities that shape how your Pact Magic and Eldritch Blast work. 20+ Invocations are available.

**Pact Boons** (available as Invocations):

**Pact of the Blade** — Summon a magical weapon as a Bonus Action. The weapon uses Charisma for attack and damage rolls. Counts as a Warlock spell attack.

**Pact of the Chain** — Gain access to a powerful familiar beyond the standard Find Familiar options. Your familiar can take the Attack action on your command.

**Pact of the Tome** — Gain a Book of Shadows granting three additional cantrips from any class list.

**Notable Invocations:**

| INVOCATION | EFFECT |
|---|---|
| **Agonizing Blast** | Add Charisma modifier to Eldritch Blast damage |
| **Repelling Blast** | Eldritch Blast pushes targets 10 ft on hit |
| **Devil's Sight** | See normally in magical and non-magical darkness |
| **Armor of Shadows** | Cast Mage Armor on yourself at will |
| **Eldritch Mind** | Advantage on Concentration saving throws |
| **Fiendish Vigor** | Cast False Life on yourself at will |
| **Mask of Many Faces** | Cast Disguise Self at will |
| **Misty Visions** | Cast Silent Image at will |
| **One with Shadows** | When in dim light or darkness, become Invisible as an Action |
| **Thirsting Blade** | Attack twice when taking the Attack action with Pact of the Blade weapon |

> *Full Invocation list with details available in [[Eldritch Invocations]].*

---

### ⚡ Magical Cunning

Gained at Level 2. Once per Long Rest, as an Action, you can recover **half your expended Pact Magic slots** (rounded up).

> *Magical Cunning is a middle-ground recovery tool — not as fast as Short Rest recovery, but available when a Short Rest isn't. In a long dungeon push without rest opportunities, Magical Cunning keeps you relevant.*

---

### 🌟 Mystic Arcanum

Gained at Level 11. Once per Long Rest, you can cast one spell of **6th level or higher** without expending a spell slot.

> *Mystic Arcanum gives the Warlock access to high-level spell power that Pact Magic normally can't reach. At Level 11, this is a free 6th-level spell once per Long Rest — on top of your regular Pact Magic.*

---

### 🗡️ Shadow Blade (New Addition)

**Shadow Blade** has been added to the Warlock spell list at **Level 2**:

- Creates a sword of solidified shadow
- Deals **2d8 Psychic damage** per hit
- Counts as a finesse weapon (Dex or Str)
- Deals bonus damage against targets in dim light or darkness
- Pairs naturally with **Devil's Sight** and **One with Shadows** Invocations

> *Shadow Blade gives Pact of the Blade Warlocks a powerful alternative weapon that synergizes with the class's darkness-adjacent identity.*

## 🎯 Playstyle Notes

The Warlock rewards players who **plan around Short Rests** and invest in Invocations that match their combat role.

What every Warlock shares:
- **Eldritch Blast** as the primary cantrip — enhanced by Invocations like Agonizing Blast and Repelling Blast into a powerful reliable damage option
- **Pact Magic** that recovers on Short Rest — always at maximum spell level
- **Invocations** that define your secondary identity beyond the Patron
- **Charisma** as the single primary stat — attack rolls, spell save DC, and social skills all flow from one investment

> *The Warlock doesn't run out of power. It manages it.*
> *Two slots is not a limitation. It's a discipline.*
> *Use them well, rest, and use them again.*
