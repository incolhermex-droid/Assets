## What Is the White Hat?

The White Hat is a **Gunslinger archetype** that embodies the law itself — a relentless, principled gunfighter who has appointed themselves enforcer of order in a chaotic world. Where the High Roller bluffs and the Spellslinger fuses magic with bullets, the White Hat brings something neither offers: unshakable resolve and zero tolerance.

This archetype trades risk and trickery for **reliability and control** — fear immunity, mental resistance, battlefield denial, and an inevitability that follows anyone the White Hat has marked.

## Level 3 Features

### ⚖️ Lay Down the Law

*Your reputation precedes you.*

You have **Advantage** on Charisma (Intimidation) checks against creatures who have attacked you or your allies in the last minute.

> *Lay Down the Law rewards staying engaged in a fight. The longer a hostile creature has been attacking your party, the more reliably you can intimidate them afterward — useful for interrogation, forcing surrender, or breaking morale mid-encounter.*

---

### 😐 Steely-Eyed

*Fear doesn't reach you. Charm doesn't either, not for long.*

You **cannot be Frightened**. Additionally, when a creature attempts to **Charm** you, you can make a **Wisdom saving throw** to resist the effect — even if the spell or ability would normally not allow one.

> *Steely-Eyed is a complete immunity to one condition and a guaranteed save chance against another that's normally inescapable. Against enemy enchanters, charm-based bosses, or fear-heavy encounters, the White Hat simply doesn't break the same way other characters do.*

![Gunslinger Divider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/incolhermex-droid/Assets/refs/heads/main/gunslinger-divider.svg)

## Level 6 Features

### ✋ Reach for the Skies

*A lawful warning. Compliance is optional. The consequences aren't.*

As a **Bonus Action**, you issue a lawful warning to a creature within 30 feet. They must succeed on a **Wisdom saving throw** or spend their **next turn with their hands raised**, unable to take actions or reactions.

> *Reach for the Skies is a single-target hard-control option that costs only a Bonus Action — meaning you can still attack, cast, or use other features the same turn you lock an enemy down. Against a single dangerous threat, this can functionally remove them from a full round of combat.*

![Gunslinger Divider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/incolhermex-droid/Assets/refs/heads/main/gunslinger-divider.svg)

## Level 10 Features

### 🎯 Long Arm of the Law

*There is no hiding from justice.*

Your firearm attacks **ignore Half Cover and Three-Quarters Cover**. Additionally, creatures you've **marked as targets** cannot benefit from the **Help action** from other creatures.

> *Long Arm of the Law removes two of the most common defensive tools in the game simultaneously — cover and ally assistance. A marked target behind a wall is still a clean shot. A marked target whose friends try to protect them gets no benefit from it. This is the capstone that makes the White Hat's "nobody escapes" identity mechanically real.*

![Gunslinger Divider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/incolhermex-droid/Assets/refs/heads/main/gunslinger-divider.svg)

## 🎯 Playstyle Summary

The White Hat is a **control-oriented enforcer** that trades the High Roller's variance and the Spellslinger's magic for unshakable consistency and battlefield denial.

**Your combat identity:**
1. **Steely-Eyed** — fear and charm-based tactics simply don't work on you; stand your ground through encounters that would break other characters
2. **Reach for the Skies** — lock down the most dangerous enemy on the field as a Bonus Action, every encounter
3. **Lay Down the Law** — post-fight, your Intimidation checks against former attackers are reliable
4. **Long Arm of the Law** at Level 10 — cover and ally support stop protecting your marked target

> *The High Roller gambles. The Spellslinger improvises. The White Hat doesn't need either — there is no cover, no fear, and no escape from the sentence already passed.*
