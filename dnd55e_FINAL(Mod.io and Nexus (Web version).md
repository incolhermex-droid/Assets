## 📖 WHAT IS THIS MOD?

Baldur's Gate 3 was built on D&D 5e (2014). This mod upgrades your entire campaign to the **2024 Player's Handbook (5.5e)** standards — not just a patch, but a full mechanical reconstruction of how the game plays.

If you've read the new PHB and wanted *that* game inside BG3, this is it.

Before touching classes or feats, the mod rebuilds core mechanics that affect every character in the game:

* 🩸 **Bloodied** — Creatures at or below 50% HP are now Bloodied. Many abilities and spells interact with this state.
* 🛡️ **Heavy Armor Strength Requirement** — Strength 13 required to move effectively in Heavy Armor. Low-STR casters can no longer wear plate freely.
* ⚡ **One Spell Slot per Turn** — You can only expend one spell slot per turn. No more action + bonus action double-casting.
* 🗡️ **Equipping Weapons** — You can now draw or equip weapons as part of your Attack action, removing the action tax.
* 🤸 **Prone Rework** — Standing from Prone costs half your Speed. Attacks from within reach have Advantage; ranged attacks have Disadvantage.
* 👁️ **Hiding Rework** — Stealth rules updated to match 2024 standards.
* ✨ **Extra Attack + War Magic** — Certain classes can now mix spells and attacks within Extra Attack sequences.
* 🏹 **Heavy Weapon Ability Requirement** — Heavy weapons now require appropriate ability scores to wield effectively.

![08_weapon_mastery](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/incolhermex-droid/Assets/c9dbfbff0ed8f4aaaaa389676acae0f50aae8305/banners/08_weapon_mastery.svg)

Every martial character now has access to **Weapon Mastery** — a set of special properties tied to specific weapon types that add a new layer of tactical decision-making to every encounter.

* 💥 **Cleave** — On a hit, make an additional attack against a second creature adjacent to the first.
* 🛡️ **Graze** — Even on a miss, deal damage equal to your ability modifier.
* 🔨 **Topple** — Force a Constitution save or knock the target Prone.
* 💨 **Nick** — The Light property allows an extra attack without consuming your Bonus Action.
* 🏹 **Vex** — On a hit, gain Advantage on your next attack against the same target.
* 🐢 **Slow** — On a hit, reduce the target's Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.
* ↩️ **Push** — On a hit, push the target 10 feet away from you.
* 😵 **Sap** — On a hit, the target has Disadvantage on its next attack roll.
* 🌀 **Flex** — Use this weapon one-handed or two-handed for different damage dice.

*Fighters, Barbarians, Paladins, Rangers, Rogues, Monks, and the new classes all gain Weapon Mastery at Level 1.*
